# Sukoon · your calm corner

**Live:** https://madhurids012-jpg.github.io/sukoon/

Sukoon (सुकून, "peace of mind") is a gentle daily check-in, built as a small installable web app.

- **Inner weather**: check in as Stormy, Rainy, Cloudy, Sunny or Glowing, with tags and a short note
- **Mood garden**: every check-in grows a flower in that day's colour, plus a 5-week grid, streaks and a 30-day weather line
- **Breathe**: guided Calm, Box and 4-7-8 breathing with an animated orb
- **Little jar of good**: save gratitude moments and pull one out on a heavy day
- **Letters to future me**: sealed until the date you choose
- **Small kindness** of the day, a daily thought, a sky that follows the time of day (or pick dawn/day/dusk/night), soft chimes and ambient rain/ocean generated in the browser
- **Private by design**: no accounts, no servers, no trackers. Everything lives in your browser's localStorage. Works offline and can be installed to your home screen (PWA).

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step.
