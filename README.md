# Sukoon · सुकून

**Live:** https://sukoon-madhuri.vercel.app/

A calm daily ritual with an Indian soul. Sukoon means "peace of mind".

- **Aaj (Today)**: check your *mann ka mausam*: Toofan, Baarish, Badal, Dhoop or Utsav, with tags and a short note
- **Rangoli**: every check-in adds one petal in your mood's colour; 30 days make one full rangoli. Plus this week's toran, streak diyas and a 30-day mood wave
- **Diya**: breathe with a diya flame that rises and settles (Shaant 4-2-6, Sama vritti 4-4-4-4, Neend 4-7-8)
- **Gullak**: drop good moments into a terracotta gullak and pull one out on a heavy day; post a *chitthi* (inland letter) to your future self
- **Aaj ki neki** (a small daily kindness) and **aaj ka vichaar** (a thought for the day), in English and Hindi
- Subah, din, shaam and raat themes that follow the time of day; temple-bell chimes and a tanpura or monsoon-rain drone, all generated in the browser
- **Private by design**: no accounts, no servers, no trackers. Data stays in your browser. Works offline and installs to your home screen.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step.
